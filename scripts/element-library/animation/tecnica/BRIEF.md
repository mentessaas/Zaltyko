# Technique research brief — Zaltyko element animations

Goal: our animations must follow the CORRECT TECHNIQUE a gymnastics coach teaches, not just
positions. Research the standard technique of each movement family assigned to you and write it
as an animator's reference: phases in order, what the body does in each phase, approximate joint
angles (shoulder, hip, knee, elbow; body shape), approximate timing (fraction of the movement or
seconds), where the centre of mass goes, typical heights/speeds, the coaching cues that define
good technique, and the typical errors that would make a coach say "that is not how it is done".

Sources: coaching manuals and federation education material (e.g. FIG Academy/Age Group
Development Programme, national federation coaching resources), biomechanics papers (cite them),
reputable coaching sites. Use web search. Record facts in your own words with the source list at
the end; do not copy text. Never use Upgrade Gymnastics or GymPro Atlas.

Write in English, concise, structured per family, with a short "animation recipe" for each family:
the key poses (absolute body description, not pixels) and the order/timing an animator should
key. Output file given in your task. Reply with a 5-line summary.
